What this is

Not a click-to-chat form. Not the web chatbot portal. This is the WhatsApp Business channel: the bot talks to buyers on the business number, runs the same qualifier brain as the AI chatbot, and only when the lead is legitimate / qualified does it forward a ticket to Ray’s personal WhatsApp.

1

2
Bot replies as Ray Same qualifier.js brain — stock, test drive, trade-in, phone.
3
Qualify Need intent + contact. Junk / incomplete stays on the bot — no ping to Ray.
4
Forward → personal WhatsApp Full ticket to Ray’s personal number so he can close the deal.
