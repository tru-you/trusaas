# TruDealer documentation

The dealer suite, module by module. Each page covers what the module does, who uses it, the core workflow, and how it connects to the rest of the stack.

## Capture & condition

- **[TruLens](./trulens.md)** — guided phone photo capture; scores the shoot and pushes it to the DMS.
- **[TruInspect](./truinspect.md)** — vehicle inspection reports (VIR) and trade-in appraisals.

## The dealer desktop

- **[TruFlow](./truflow.md)** — the DMS: stock, sales pipeline, leads, finance and recon, deal readiness.
- **[TruFlow Light](./truflow-light.md)** — a lightweight console on the same DMS: leads, inventory, vehicle upload.

## Website & buyer engagement

- **[Website widgets](./widgets.md)** — the embeddable options at a glance (TruChat, TruAfford, TruRepay, TruForm).
- **[TruChat](./truchat.md)** — the 24/7 website assistant: answers, stock search, bookings, lead capture.
- **[TruAfford (Finance)](./truafford.md)** — the affordability widget embedded on dealer sites.
- **[TruForm](./truform.md)** — the embeddable enquiry form; captures leads into the DMS.
- **[TruRepay](./trurepay.md)** — the on-page finance calculator that files a lead with the figures.
- **[Showrooms](./showrooms.md)** — TruLive (live buyer walkthrough), TruTrade (live trade-in appraisal), TruOrbit (360° spin).

## Setup

- **[Integrations](./integrations.md)** — the stock feed, webhooks, lead endpoints, and what's not wired up.
- **[Getting Started](./getting-started.md)** — onboarding a dealer from first login to a car live on the website.
