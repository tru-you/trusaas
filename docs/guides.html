One code, one dealership

You sign into every TruDealer app with the same dealer access code, issued for your dealership in TruFlow. TruLens and TruInspect can each be used on their own; TruFlow Mobile is both the Premium companion and a standalone app for smaller dealers. Whatever you use, the guide for it is above.
