Terms of Service & Credit Wallet Agreement

1. Credit Wallet Economy

TruData operates strictly on a credit-wallet transaction model. 1 Credit equals R13.27 on PAYG tiers down to R5.83 on Enterprise tiers. Purchased credits never expire and can be used across all 5 data pillars.

2. Non-Refundable Data Policy

Due to real-time API verification costs incurred with external providers (TransUnion eValue8, Serper, Bright Data), credit deductions are non-refundable once an extraction query is executed.

3. Data Accuracy & Verification

Data provided by TruData is aggregated directly from official public registers (CIPC, Deeds Office) and licensed bureau partners (TransUnion South Africa). TruData does not alter third-party financial records.

Privacy Policy & POPIA Compliance Statement

1. Protection of Personal Information (POPIA)

TruData complies fully with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We do not collect, store, or sell consumer personal tracking data.

2. Bank Verification Security (SafePay AVS)

SafePay TransUnion Account Verification Service (AVS) queries are transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 channels directly to TransUnion. Bank account numbers are never logged or stored on persistent disk.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

TruData uses browser LocalStorage strictly to persist your wallet balance and session email context. Zero cross-site tracking cookies are used.
