00 Call Playbook

Scripts. Openers. Closes.

No pressure, no pitch vomit. One goal: get them on a 25-minute walkthrough. We are a management platform, not a finance provider — we never touch their money or hold their data hostage. Adapt these to the conversation.

THE OPENER — SAY THIS FIRST, EVERY CALL
THE HONESTY OPEN

"Hi [Name], I'm going to be honest — this is a cold call, and I hate making them as much as you hate getting them. Can I have 30 seconds to explain while I've got you?"

Disarms the reflex to hang up before you've said anything. The honesty buys you the 30 seconds — then roll straight into whichever opener below fits the dealer. Use this on every cold call, indie or VMG, before anything else.

QUALIFYING QUESTIONS
Q1 · INVENTORY
"How many vehicles do you typically have in stock at any given time?"
Determines tier fit. Single rooftop under ~30 units → TruStart. 20–80/mo → TruPro. Group/multi-branch → TruEnterprise.
Q2 · CURRENT SYSTEM
"Are you running any DMS or website right now — VMG, something custom, or nothing at all?"
On VMG/legacy → land-alongside angle, zero switching risk. Nothing → TruStart is the whole foundation, easy close.
Q3 · LEADS
"How do you currently manage leads — WhatsApp, email, phone, a system?"
No system → TruFlow DMS + TruForms is the anchor. WhatsApp only → TruChat AI is the pain-point close.
Q4 · TRUST / CONDITION
"When you sell or take in a trade, do you document the vehicle's condition in any formal way?"
No → TruInspect is the free-in-every-tier hook, always demo this. Yes but manual → still an easy upgrade sell.
Q5 · DECISION MAKER
"Are you the right person for a tech decision like this, or is there someone else I should loop in?"
Find the DM early. Group/franchise → GM or ops lead. Owner-operator → they're usually on the call already.
Q6 · PAIN
"What's your biggest operational headache right now — stock, leads, trust, or admin?"
Open-ended. Let them talk. Map their answer to the right module and lead the demo there.

OBJECTION REBUTTALS

Don't argue. Acknowledge, reframe, pull back to the demo. Every rebuttal ends with a question that books time.

PRICE / BUDGET
"It's too expensive"

"Fair — but TruStart is zero setup fee and R2,499 a month, and TruInspect, your website, and unlimited stock are all included. Quick question though: how many hours a week does your team spend on manual admin and follow-ups right now? This replaces that. It pays for itself on one extra sale a month. Let me show you the platform live — 15 minutes, then you decide."

Reframe cost as cost-of-doing-nothing. Anchor to one extra sale.

"I can't afford the TruPro setup fee"

"Understood — that's exactly why TruStart exists. Zero setup, R2,499/month, get the foundation live and TruInspect running day one. When you're ready to add the full DMS, F&I, and video handover, you upgrade to TruPro without re-onboarding. Can I show you what TruStart actually gets you first?"

Pivot to start-small / scale path. Lowers the entry barrier, keeps the door open to TruPro later.

INCUMBENT / STALL
"We already use VMG / [other DMS]"

"Good — so you already know the value of a proper system. I'm not asking you to rip anything out. We sit alongside it — trade-in appraisals, AI chat, condition reports — the gaps most DMS platforms don't cover. Worst case you confirm you're covered. Best case you find a real gap. 15 minutes — when are you free?"

Never knock the incumbent. Position as a gap-check, not a rip-out. This is the entire land-alongside strategy.

"Just email me something"

"I'll send the packages page, no problem. But honestly, a PDF won't show you the part that matters — watching your own stock live in the platform, and TruChat answering a real enquiry in real time. That's 15 minutes on screen and it'll tell you more than any email. Can I lock a slot this week, and I'll send the overview to confirm it?"

Agree to send, but trade the email for a booked slot. Email confirms the demo, doesn't replace it.

"No time right now / call me later"

"That's exactly why I'm calling — the platform's built to give you time back, not take it. I won't hold you now. Give me 25 minutes Thursday or Friday and I'll show you only the parts relevant to your operation. Which one works?"

Don't accept the open-ended "later." Offer two named slots and close on choice.

CURVEBALL
"Can you build me something custom?"

"TruEnterprise covers custom workflows and API access — but before we talk custom, let me show you what's already in the platform, because most of what dealers think they need custom is already built. We'll demo the standard modules, then map only the real gaps to an Enterprise quote. That way you're not paying to build what already exists. 20 minutes — when works?"

Never quote custom cold. Demo standard first, scope gaps after. Protects time and MRR.

"I'm not the decision maker"

"No problem — who handles the website and stock side? I'd rather show you both at once so nothing gets lost in translation. Can you get them on a 25-minute screen share with you this week? I'll keep it tight and relevant."

Get the real DM into the same demo. Don't relay through a gatekeeper.

00·B Pre-Demo Prep

Walk in knowing.

Ten minutes per dealer, the evening before. You're not writing a dossier — you're picking the modules to open with so the walkthrough lands as "you built this for me."

1 · STOCK COUNT & MIX

Count vehicles on their site or Facebook. Single rooftop, under 30 → TruStart, keep it lean. 20–80/mo → TruPro. Group/multi-branch → TruEnterprise, lead multi-branch + API.

Mix matters: higher-end stock → TruInspect + 3D Orbit (trust on expensive units). Volume budget stock → TruLens guided capture (speed).

2 · CURRENT SYSTEM

On VMG? A legacy DMS? Nothing at all? On an incumbent → land-alongside is your headline pull, zero switching risk. On nothing → the full TruStart foundation is the gap.

Strongest single positioning line. Never pitch a rip-out — always a gap-fill.

3 · WEBSITE STATE

Open it. Slow, broken, no finance calculator, not mobile → lead with TruShowroom + TruRepay. Decent site already → skip it, go straight to TruChat / TruInspect.

Don't pitch what they're happy with. A clean site means the gap is elsewhere.

4 · LEAD HANDLING

WhatsApp number shown? Contact form only? Nothing after hours? WhatsApp-led (most SA indies) → TruChat AI + TruForms. They run sales on WhatsApp and lose leads overnight.

Near-universal pain, even on dealers with a full DMS. Almost always lands as an opener.

5 · DECISION-MAKER & SIZE

Owner-operator → they're on the demo, close in the room. Group → ask who else needs to be on the call before you demo.

Never single-thread a group deal. Get every decision-maker into the same call.

A tailored 25-minute walkthrough closes. A full-stack tour bores. Tailor, then stop.
