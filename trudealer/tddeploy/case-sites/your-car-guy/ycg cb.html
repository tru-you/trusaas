Showroom Assistant Platform

An automated sales assistant designed for pre-owned car dealerships. It standardizes client inquiries, logs user pathway interactions, handles trade-in profiles, and schedules showroom test drives.

🛡️ Live Portfolio Sync

Matches active query keywords directly against real available inventory (bakkies, SUVs, hatchbacks) to present matching entries.

🔄 Trade-In Valuations

Processes vehicle criteria (Year, Model, Mileage, Condition) and transitions directly to scheduling a physical appraisal slot.

📱 WhatsApp Lead Dispatch

Allows showroom operators to package visitor trace paths and send them directly to sales representatives on WhatsApp with one click.

📅 Sunday Exclusion

The scheduler automatically skips Sundays, ensuring all appointment options map to standard operational hours.

⏰ Saturday Adjusted Slots

Recognizes weekend operations to limit Saturday bookings to 07:30 - 13:00, preventing out-of-bounds schedules.

🔒 Local Storage Persist

User pathway checkpoints and contact details are stored within the session state, protecting entries from browser refreshes.

📡 Real-time Database Sync

Simulates continuous AJAX transmissions, ensuring local dashboards update instantly as a user navigates the assistant.
