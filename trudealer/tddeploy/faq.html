The TruDealer Platform

What is TruDealer?

TruDealer is an all-in-one vertical SaaS platform built specifically for independent dealerships in South Africa. It brings together vehicle photography (TruLens PWA), condition reports with inspector e-signatures (TruInspect), a full dealer management system with deal jackets (TruFlow), floor staff mobile companion (TruFlow Mobile), AI WhatsApp sales agent (TruChat), and high-converting website widgets into a unified monthly subscription.

Is TruDealer a full DMS or just an inspection tool?

Both. The TruPro and TruEnterprise tiers include a complete cloud DMS (TruFlow) with stock management, OTP deal jackets (Offer to Purchase, Tax Invoice, Delivery Handover, Proforma), commission calculations, settlement tracking, and Codat cloud accounting sync (Xero, QuickBooks, Sage).

Do my staff need to install an app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store?

No. TruLens and TruInspect are Progressive Web Apps (PWAs). They run directly in the mobile browser (Chrome, Safari) on any Android phone or iPhone, install to the home screen with one tap, and work offline even in basement parking lots or remote areas with zero cell signal.

💳 Pricing, Fees & Contracts

How much does TruDealer cost?

TruDealer offers two transparent monthly plans:

  • TruStart (R1,599/month): Single rooftop, unlimited stock units, PWA photo capture studio, condition reports, AI chat, and website integration. Zero setup fee.
  • TruPro (R3,599/month): Full DMS, OTP deal jackets, live market scraper valuation, all 8 standalone widgets, and Codat accounting. Setup is R4,500 (R2,250 if signed within 48h of demo).
  • TruEnterprise (R3,599/rooftop/month): Multi-branch dealer groups, custom white-label, dedicated API endpoints, SLA, and voice AI.
Are there long-term contracts or lock-ins?

No. TruDealer operates on a month-to-month license. You can cancel anytime without cancellation penalties. Your vehicle data and inspection reports can be exported at any time.

Do you charge extra per user or per seat?

No per-seat fees on photo capture or trade-in appraisals. All your yard photographers and workshop inspectors can capture and inspect stock without buying extra licenses.

TransUnion eValue8 & Imagin8 Lookups

What TransUnion calls are free vs chargeable?

TruDealer provides unlimited flat-fee make and model catalogue queries (getModels) and vehicle technical specifications (getStaticInfo). Chargeable checks (TransUnion retail/trade valuations, Natis police/finance registration clearance, and accident insurance claims history) are bundle-gated and deduct from your dealership's pre-funded bundle balance.

How do bundle top-ups work?

Dealership admins can top up check bundles directly from TruFlow Premium. Bundles are stored in a central ledger and accessible across all connected apps (TruLens and TruInspect) seamlessly.

️ Multi-Vertical Support (Motorcycles, Boats, Trucks, Caravans)

Can I use TruDealer for a motorcycle dealership?

Yes. TruDealer Moto is fully live across all 4 apps. It includes a 14-angle motorcycle photo studio, 6-phase motorcycle VIR (fork seals, frame integrity, chain/sprocket wear, 2-tyre depth), TransUnion superbike catalogue lookups, and dedicated "Book a Demo Ride" website widgets.

What about boats, commercial trucks, and caravans?

Following TruDealer Moto, we have planned dedicated verticals for TruDealer Marine (boats & jetskis), TruDealer Commercial (heavy trucks & yellow metal), and TruDealer Caravans (campers & trailers). These are on our product roadmap and dealerships can register for early access.

Can a dealership sell multiple verticals simultaneously?

Yes. If your yard sells both cars and motorcycles, TruDealer automatically selects the appropriate 28-angle car or 14-angle motorcycle template based on the vehicle make and stock category.

🛡️ Onboarding, Legal & CPA Compliance

How fast can our yard go live?

Most dealerships are up and running within 24 to 48 hours. Because TruDealer runs on cloud PWA architecture, there is zero software to install on dealer PCs. We import your active inventory list via spreadsheet or automated feed so you don't lose a day of business.

How does TruDealer protect dealers under the Consumer Protection Act (CPA)?

Every TruInspect VIR condition report generates a permanent, timestamped PDF documenting all pre-existing cosmetic flaws, mechanical checks, and tyre depths, signed off with the inspector's drawn e-signature. This provides unshakeable proof of vehicle condition at point of sale, preventing voetstoots disputes.
