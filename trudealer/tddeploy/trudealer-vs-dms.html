TruDealer vs traditional DMS

Traditional DMS software was built for franchise dealers. TruDealer is a dealer operating system for independents — website, inspections, AI chat and DMS in one monthly license.

By Paul de Beer, Founder4 min read
If you're an independent dealer shopping for software, you'll see two very different categories. Legacy DMS platforms handle the backend of a franchise operation. TruDealer was built for how independents actually sell — online, on WhatsApp, on condition reports and on fast responses.

What a traditional DMS is

A dealer management system (DMS) runs the operational backbone of a dealership: deals, F&I, inventory, service and parts. The big names were built decades ago for franchise networks — fixed processes, big implementation projects, and per-module pricing. They're powerful at what they do, but they stop at the showroom door: they don't run your website, take your car's photos, appraise a trade-in on video, or answer a WhatsApp lead at 9pm.

The honest part

If you run a franchise used department or a large group with deep processes, a heavyweight DMS makes sense — and TruEnterprise includes API access so you can run TruDealer alongside it. But for the independent dealer moving 5–80 units a month, paying separately for a DMS, a website, an inspection tool and a chat widget is exactly the old way of doing things. TruDealer bundles them for less than most DMS subscriptions alone — from R1,599/month with no setup fee.
