00 · Before you start

What it is & who it's for

TruFlow Mobile is a phone app — it installs to your home screen and runs full-screen. Which role it plays depends on your dealership.

◎ Companion
If your dealership runs TruFlow Premium on the desktop, this is your floor companion — everything you do here syncs both ways with Premium.
◐ Standalone
For a smaller dealer it's the whole system on a phone — leads, stock and deals, no desktop required.
Your phone
Any modern phone browser. Open app.tru-saas.com — on a laptop it shows a QR code to open it on your phone instead (or a link to TruFlow Premium for the full desktop DMS).
Your dealer code
One code — the same one you use on desktop and across the TruDealer apps. Your dealer principal issues it; there's no self-signup.
TruLens for photos
You add a car's details here, but its photos are shot in TruLens — a car saves unpublished until it has a gallery.

01 · Get it on your phone

Install it on your phone

Add it to your home screen once and it opens full-screen like any app, ready on the floor.

  1. Open the app

    In your phone browser, go to app.tru-saas.com.

  2. Add to home screen

    iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. Android (Chrome): tap the install banner, or the menu (⋮) → Install app.

  3. Open it from the icon

    Launch TruFlow from its new home-screen icon — full-screen, no browser bar, and it works offline for the app shell.

On a laptop?

Above a certain width the app shows a hand-off screen instead — “Your TruFlow Mobile companion — manage leads, stock and deals from the showroom floor” — with the app.tru-saas.com link, Copy link, Email the link to yourself and a QR code to scan. For the full desktop DMS, open TruFlow Premium instead.

02 · Get in

Sign in

The opening screen is the TruFlow · Mobile wordmark and one field.

  1. Enter your dealer code

    Type it into Dealer code.

  2. Tap “Sign in”

    It shows Signing in…, then drops you on Home. If it says “That code isn't recognised,” check the code with your dealer principal; “Can't reach the server” just means try again with signal.

Shared handset?

Signing out clears everything cached on the device — leads and stock included — so the next person to sign in never sees another dealer's customers.

04 · Your morning read

Home — your floor at a glance

Home greets you by dealership — “Your floor at a glance” — then lays out your day.

  • Your numbers — a grid of the KPIs that matter today.
  • Follow up — the leads that need you, with All leads → into the full pipeline.
  • Recent stock — your latest units, with Manage → into Stock.

Pull down to refresh at any time.

05 · Job A

Leads

Every enquiry in one list, with the fastest possible path to a reply.

Find & filter

Search with Search leads, filter by stage, and sort (Newest / Oldest / Name). The stages are:

AllNewContactedNegotiatingWon

Tap Walk-in to capture someone who's just walked onto the floor — a new lead in seconds.

Work a lead

  1. Open the lead sheet

    Tap a lead and its detail slides up. Up top: Call and WhatsApp (one tap each), the enquiry message, and the car they're interested in.

  2. Move it along

    Use the status stepper — New → Contacted → Negotiating → Won — tapping the stage the lead has reached.

  3. Log what happened

    Drop a note (“What happened? e.g. Left a voicemail, will try Friday”) so the timeline stays honest, then Save. It syncs straight to Premium.

Reaching Negotiating matters

When a lead hits Negotiating, its deal opens back in TruFlow Premium's Deal Readiness — so the desk can start the paperwork while you keep working the floor.

06 · Job B

Stock

Your inventory in your hand — search it, check a price, publish or pull a car.

Find a car

Search with Search make, model, stock #, filter (All In stock Live Sold), and sort by newest, price or mileage. Each card shows a Live or Unpublished pill, the price and quick spec chips.

Open a car

Tap a car for its detail sheet:

Website toggle
Flip it on to publish the car to your website, off to pull it.
Status
Segmented control: In stock / Sold.
Pricing
The asking price alongside TruPrice, so you can see where it sits.
Save / delete
Save commits changes; the delete button retires the unit.

07 · A new arrival

Add a vehicle

The Add tab is a short two-part form — the car, then the money.

  1. The car

    Year, Make, Model, Variant / trim, Body, Fuel, Transmission and Mileage (km).

  2. The money

    Asking price (R) and TruPrice (R).

  3. Add to inventory

    Tap Add to inventory. The car saves unpublished — “shoot in TruLens to go live.” Open TruLens, shoot that stock number's guided slots and export; the gallery flows back and you can publish it.

08 · The rest

Activity & Account

Activity

A running timeline — “What's happening across your dealership” — grouped by Today / Yesterday: new leads, vehicles published, photos uploaded, deals closed, price changes. It's the shared heartbeat of the floor.

Account

Your profile with your dealer code shown back (the one string you read out to support), a Support row that opens WhatsApp, and Sign out. Signing out clears the device's cached data.

09 · When you're stuck

Getting help

Two helpers live in the top bar, and neither leaves the app.

? How do I…?
Short job walkthroughs tuned to your screen (the main one badged Start here), each a few numbered steps — with a WhatsApp “Still stuck? Contact support” at the bottom.
✦ Dealer Assist
“Ask me about stock, leads, pricing — whatever you need on the floor.” Tap a suggestion (best seller, leads this week, stock over 60 days) or type your own.
Rule of thumb

The two tabs you'll live in are Leads and Stock. Chase the enquiry, keep the car's record straight — everything else on the phone supports those two, and it all shows up on the desktop the moment you save.
