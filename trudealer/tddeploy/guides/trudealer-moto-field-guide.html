Motorcycle Dealership Operational Playbook

How independent motorcycle dealerships use TruDealer to shoot studio photos in 14 angles, conduct 6-phase bike inspections with 2-tyre grading, and capture trade-ins online.

Step 01

Setting Up TruDealer for Motorcycles

TruDealer automatically detects motorcycle makes (Harley-Davidson, BMW Motorrad, Ducati, KTM, Yamaha, Honda, Kawasaki, Suzuki, Triumph, etc.). When your dealership profile is set to moto:

  • TruLens dynamically switches to the 14-angle motorcycle photo workflow.
  • TruInspect loads the 6-phase motorcycle VIR inspection checklist with a 2-tyre assessment.
  • Drop-in website widgets switch to "Book a Demo Ride" and motorcycle trade-in valuation modes.
Step 02

The 14-Angle PWA Photo Workflow (TruLens)

TruLens guides yard staff around the bike in under 3 minutes with zero camera viewfinder clutter:

  • 1. Front 3/4 Left & Right: Full stance and headlight cluster.
  • 2. Full Profiles (Left & Right): Stance, fairings, and ground clearance.
  • 3. Front Wheel, Brake & Fork: Disc rotor wear, caliper status, and fork seal integrity.
  • 4. Cockpit & Handlebars: Mirrors, switchgear, clutch/brake levers, and grips.
  • 5. Odometer: Digital or analogue instrument cluster displaying current reading.
  • 6. Fuel Tank & Controls: Top tank paint condition, fuel cap, and steering damper.
  • 7. Engine Casings (Left & Right): Alternator cover, clutch cover, and drop scuffs.
  • 8. Exhaust System: Header pipes, heat shields, and silencer condition.
  • 9. Rear Wheel, Chain & Sprocket: Chain slack, sprocket teeth wear, and rear tyre tread.
  • 10. Tail & Plate: Rear light assembly, tail tidy, and licence plate.
  • 11. VIN Plate: Frame neck VIN stamping for verification.
Step 03

6-Phase Motorcycle VIR Inspection (TruInspect)

Protect your dealership against Consumer Protection Act (CPA) disputes by generating a signed digital condition report before delivery:

  • Phase 1 — Frame & Controls: Steering head bearings, fork alignment, and frame straightness.
  • Phase 2 — Engine & Powertrain: Cold start, oil level, clutch engagement, and coolant status.
  • Phase 3 — Wheels & Suspension: Front/rear tyre tread depth (mm), fork seals, and rear shock dampening.
  • Phase 4 — Electrical & Lighting: Headlamp high/low, indicators, brake light, and horn.
  • Phase 5 — Cosmetics & Exhaust: Drop/slide scuffs, exhaust baffles, and paint chips.
  • Phase 6 — Final Road Test: Gear shift smoothness, acceleration, and ABS braking feel.
Step 04

TransUnion Superbike Valuations & Trade-Ins

When valuing trade-ins, TruDealer queries TransUnion eValue8 for live superbike specs and book values. Website visitors can also value their current bike online using the tru-value widget embedded on your site.
