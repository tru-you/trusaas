00 · Before you start

Where it runs & who it's for

TruFlow Premium is the full DMS, built for a desktop or tablet — that's where you'll run stock, leads, deals and the books. Open a phone browser to it and it sends you to the companion app instead.

Desktop / tablet
Go to https://premium.tru-saas.com in any modern browser. The deep workflows — Deal Readiness and Documents (DocHub) — need the room a desktop or tablet gives, so they're desktop/tablet jobs.
Phone
On a phone the site hands you straight to the companion app at app.tru-saas.com — see TruFlow on your phone.
Your access code
One dealer code — the same code across the TruDealer apps. Your dealer principal issues it; there's no self-signup.
Your role
What you see depends on your role. Salespeople get the floor: stock, leads, deals, documents. Managers / owners also get Stock health, Team & Users and Settings, and can see cost prices.

01 · Get in

Sign in

One field, no password. The splash reads “Dealer management — stock, leads, F&I and reporting.”

  1. Enter your access code

    Type it into Dealership access code (“Enter your code”). Tap the eye to reveal it; a note warns if Caps Lock is on.

  2. Choose whether to stay signed in

    Tick Keep me signed in on this device on your own machine; leave it off on a shared one.

  3. Tap “Enter”

    It shows Checking…, then lands you on your Overview. If it says “Sign-in failed. Try again,” the code is wrong or not set up — check with your dealer principal.

03 · Your morning read

Dashboard (Overview)

The landing screen — “Dealership overview” — is a triage board. Read it top to bottom and you know what needs you before you've touched a menu. New inventory at the top jumps straight to Add vehicle.

The five numbers

Needs replyDue todayIn stockSoldGross after recon

Each tile carries a one-line read underneath — “oldest {wait}” or “all clear,” “{n} late” or “on track,” “{n} aged” or “fresh,” “{pct}% moved,” “{n} deals.” They're your priorities in five glances.

Below the numbers

  • Today's activity — counters for test drives, visits, trade-ins and new leads; click any to jump to it.
  • Leads — the newest enquiries with Open Pipelines and a Review File on each row.
  • Recent Showroom Inventory — latest units with age (60+ days flagged amber) and View Database.
  • Web readiness — either “all cars have galleries” or counters for not online, no photos, short and ready, with Manage into Web Management.

04 · Journey A

Stock & inventory

Everything about the cars on your floor: create them, shoot them, get them web-ready, publish them, and sell them. Photos are never captured here — they're shot in TruLens and flow back automatically.

Add a vehicle

  1. Pick a showroom category

    On Add vehicle, leave Auto — decide from price & model and your website guesses the tier, or set Premium Used / Premium Select / Premium Performance to override.

  2. Fill the specs and price

    Complete the Showroom Vehicle Specifications (year, make, model, trim, engine, transmission, fuel, body), then Retail Price (ZAR), Cost Price (ZAR), Mileage (km) and a Stock Number. The stock number is how the car is matched everywhere else — get it right.

  3. Publish to Active Stock

    Tap Publish to Active Stock and the unit lands on the floor. If you run TruLens, it offers to open TruLens to shoot this stock number now — photos come from there via Export to DMS and appear against the car automatically.

Bringing a floor across?

Bulk Import takes a CSV — “switch from VMG, an AutoTrader export, or your own spreadsheet.” Drop the file, map the columns, done.

The vehicle list

Filter the floor by status (All In stock Sold Archived), by photos (All Needs shoot Partial Web-ready) and by age (Any 30+ 60+ 90+). Each row has a Web toggle to publish/unpublish, a status badge, and quick actions. The toolbar's Export stock list CSV downloads TruFlow_Stock_List_{date}.csv — a snapshot of right now, for the bank, an audit or a buyer.

Open a car — the vehicle detail

Click any car for its detail, with tabs specs recon publish docs extras. Here you'll find:

Readiness badge
Six or more photos reads Gallery ready for web; fewer reads Shoot in TruLens before publishing.
Replace a bad photo
Tap a photo, then the trash button — “Remove this image from showroom listing?” It's off the listing at once, no round-trip to TruLens just to drop one frame. Only reshoot in TruLens if you need a replacement.
Publish toggle
The footer switch flips between Live on website and Not on website.
Remove from stock
Retires the unit (with the safeguards below).

Get a car web-ready

All Vehicles carries the gallery work: photo filter chips — Needs shoot, Partial, Web-ready — and a camera button per row that opens TruLens for that stock number. Complete the guided slots, Export to DMS, then refresh — the readiness chip turns green. The dashboard's Web readiness card keeps the counters. Your public stock feed lives at /api/public/stock?dealer=your-slug — that is exactly what your website receives. Web Management — reachable from that card's Manage button — is the power-user grid: inline-edit every field and flip Online per row, changes saving as you go.

Sell it, or pull it back

Mark sold
On the card or detail. If more than one lead is still open on the car, you're asked which deal actually closed. The car flips to SOLD, its deal closes as Closed Won, and it's unpublished from your website automatically — no calls on a car that's gone.
Return to stock
Flips a sold car back to inventory, re-lists it on your website and reopens its deal.
Archive
A car with closed deals or signed docs can't be deleted — archive it instead. It leaves the active list but stays in your sold/money figures.
Feed your stock anywhere

The public stock feed is a live API — publish or unpublish a car and it reflects immediately, no export step. Your dealer website already reads it; aggregators or your own tools can call the same URL and get JSON with every published vehicle, photos included.

05 · Journey B begins

Leads & CRM

Every enquiry, in one pipeline, with the next step never more than a click away. The header tells you where you stand: “{n} overdue · {n} due today · {n} open.”

The pipeline

Switch between Board (kanban) and List. The stages run:

New→Contacted→Test Drive Scheduled→Negotiating→Closed WonClosed Lost

Cards show an intent badge — Hot (≥80), Warm (≥50), Cold — the linked car, the source, and the next step (with a ● when overdue). WA opens WhatsApp; Done marks contacted and sets the next step. Auto-assign new shares fresh leads out to the team.

Work a lead

  1. Open the Customer File

    Review opens the lead — “Customer File — {name}” — with tabs Overview, Digital Journey Tracker, Automated Dispatches, Comms Timeline, Tasks & Follow-up, Deal Sheet, Readiness, and (on desktop) Compliance.

  2. Reply and log the next step

    Use the composer — Send template, pick how to Send by, or draft a reply with AI — then Dispatch Outbound Message. Log a call or WhatsApp with an outcome (Connected No Answer Voicemail) so the timeline stays honest.

  3. Move the stage

    Set the status and Save Status Changes. Anything past its next-action date shows as due on the dashboard so nothing goes quiet.

Add a lead by hand

New lead captures First/Last name, Phone, Email, the vehicle, an Ad Source (Website Form / Walk-in Showroom / Facebook Lead Gen / AutoTrader) and requirement notes, then Commit Lead File. There's also a public “Dealership Enquiry” form for remote capture.

Showroom Diary

Showroom Diary is your bookings calendar — test drives, viewings and trade-in appraisals, including bookings that come off your website. Summary tiles show Today / Upcoming / Completed / No-Shows. Booking a test drive can push its lead to Test Drive Scheduled; completing one moves it to Negotiating — which is exactly when the deal appears in Deal Readiness.

Ask instead of hunting

Assist (top bar) opens Dealer Assist: “Ask me what to chase, what a unit should be priced at, or what's sitting too long.” It can see your stock and leads — try “What's over 60 days?” or “How many new leads this week?”

06 · Journey B · close it  Desktop / tablet

Deals & documents

Once a lead reaches Negotiating, the deal shows up here. Two linked screens carry it home: Deal Readiness (the checklist) and Documents / DocHub (the paperwork). Both are desktop/tablet jobs.

Deal Readiness

A deal lands on Deal Readiness the moment its lead hits Negotiating. Work the checklist and watch the progress bar fill:

NATISRoadworthyInvoicedDepositDelivered

Set the Finance state alongside it — N/A Submitted Approved Declined. A thin five-segment strip shows the DocHub stages, with Open Compliance Hub to jump in. An amber triangle next to an item means the checklist and the paperwork disagree — e.g. “Compliance is signed off, but NATIS is un-ticked” — open DocHub to resolve it.

Documents — the DocHub

The document pipeline runs five stages, in order:

Proforma→Offer to Purchase→Compliance→Invoice→Handover
  1. Work each stage

    For each stage, Generate the document, Upload a signed copy, Export CSV (or Send to accounting if you've connected a package), or Skip with a reason (logged in the audit trail). The Invoice stage can Issue via Imagin8. Compliance is tick-box only — check NATIS in hand and Roadworthy certificate on file, then Confirm compliance.

  2. Sign & finalise

    Tap Sign & finalise, then Type name or Draw the signature and Confirm & finalise. If it blocks you, a required field is missing — a proforma needs the VIN and price breakdown, for example — the amber “required” list tells you exactly what to fill.

  3. Finish the deal

    Advance all five stages. When Handover is done the deal reads Complete — handed over {date} and drops off the active Documents list.

One action, everything moves

When the car is finally Mark sold, its deal closes as Closed Won and it comes off the website in the same tap. The paperwork you did here is what makes that clean — and what an audit will want to see.

07 · Know your money

Finance, recon & stock health

Three tools tell you where the money is: a live Repayment calculator to quote a buyer, Finance & Recon for the books, and Stock health for the capital tied up on your floor.

Repayment calculator

Model a finance deal on the spot — it's seeded with a car's price so you can start fast. Set:

Vehicle Total Price (ZAR)
The on-the-road price.
Downpayment / Deposit (ZAR)
What's going down.
Interest rate (%)
The financing rate.
Term & Balloon
Drag the Term (12–84 months) and Balloon (0–40%) sliders to match the deal on the table.

The Monthly Installment updates live, with Net Principal Aggregate Interest Balloon Value Lifetime Total Cost beside it. Nothing to save — it's a working calculator.

Finance & Recon

The ledger, in five tabs: Profit & Loss (Sales Revenue, Cost of Sales, Gross Profit, Operating Expenses, Net Income — with a Log Expense form), Deal Profit (deals closed, 1st and total gross), Stock Aging (units, avg days, floor value, over-60), Reconditioning (per-vehicle recon tasks and totals) and Floor Plan (exposure and interest at your floor-plan rate). If you've connected accounting, reconciled expenses and signed invoices flow to your books.

Stock health Manager / owner

The one screen that answers “what is my stock costing me?” Four headline numbers:

Capital in stockProjected marginRealised marginTied up over 60 days

Ageing bars split your capital across bands (Under 30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / Over 90 days), and the Oldest stock first table lists units worst-first — click a row to open the car and act (reprice, push on social, or move it). Aged stock is the cheapest margin you'll ever recover.

Commission & accounting

The Commission estimate (on the Team screen) projects the sales pool from closed-deal margins under three models — Flat % Volume-Based Margin-Tiered. To keep the books current, Settings lets you turn on Accounting and connect Xero QuickBooks Zoho Books via a secure popup, so the DocHub Invoice stage pushes straight through instead of exporting a CSV.

08 · Get it seen

Media, web & TruSocial

Your website reads TruFlow directly, and — with TruSocial switched on — a car can go to your social channels from its own page.

Post a car to social & marketplace

  1. Open the car's Social & Share tab

    Every vehicle in your stock includes a dedicated Social & Share command hub. It instantly generates tailored copy and hashtags for 6 major platforms: Facebook Marketplace, Facebook Page, Instagram, WhatsApp Status, LinkedIn, and Google Business Profile.

  2. 1-Click Copy & Deep-Link

    Tap Copy to copy the tailored text with compliant specs and pricing, then tap the platform button to launch straight into that composer. For Facebook Marketplace, tap Download 8-Photo Pack to get your studio photos instantly ready to drag and drop.

  3. Zero token expiry

    No fragile third-party OAuth connections or expired API keys — you publish with 100% native platform fidelity and zero middleman friction.

You can also share straight from your dealer website: open the car and tap Share for WhatsApp, Facebook, Copy link, or your device's full share sheet. It shares the car's live listing page, so the buyer lands on the real thing.

09 · Your people  Manager / owner

Team & Users

Give each salesperson or manager their own login, scoped to your dealership. The screen shows everyone with access and, per person, a way to reissue or revoke.

  1. Add staff

    Add staff opens the form. Note it adds a billable seat.

  2. Enter their details

    Full Name, E-mail Address, Account Role (Salesperson or Manager) and Contact Number, then Add & issue code. They'll only ever see this dealership's stock and leads.

  3. Hand over the code

    Copy the one-time access code and give it to them — it won't be shown again. Tap Done. Later, New code rotates it and Remove access revokes it (Restore brings them back).

10 · Set it up once  Manager / owner

Settings & branding

What Settings holds is what makes your documents and listings look like you. Fill it once and everything downstream inherits it.

Dealer details
Registered name, Trading as, Registration & VAT numbers, contact email, website and address — “used on invoices, agreements and your listing.” Plus your Imagin8 / TransUnion API key.
Document settings
Your logo, banking details, sale terms, invoice-extra defaults, OTP terms, invoice numbering (prefix + next number, e.g. INV-0001), ownership & warranty clauses and a footer note — the content baked into generated documents.
Accounting
Turn it on and connect Xero, QuickBooks or Zoho Books. Turning it off never deletes a connection.
TruSocial
Turn it on and connect Facebook, Instagram and Google Business (shown when the TruSocial add-on is enabled for you).
Add-ons

Beyond the core DMS, your dealership can have TruLens (capture), TruInspect (condition reports), TruSocial (social posting), TruLive / TruValue and the TruFlow Mobile companion switched on. Which you have is set for your dealership — features you don't have simply won't show.

11 · Off the desk

TruFlow on your phone

Premium lives on desktop and tablet — but there's a phone app that goes where you go.

Open app.tru-saas.com on your phone and sign in with the same dealer code. Add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on iOS, or the install banner on Android) and it runs full-screen like a native app. Leads, stock, walk-in adds and activity all sync both ways with your desktop Premium.

◎ On the floor
For Premium dealers it's the companion — capture a walk-in, reply to a lead, check a price without going back to the desk.
◐ On its own
For smaller dealers it also runs standalone — the whole show on a phone, no desktop required.

12 · When you're stuck

Getting help

Three levels, closest first — none of them leave TruFlow.

? Guides — “How do I…?”
The top-bar Guides button. Job-based walkthroughs tuned to your screen, badged Start here for the main journey, each printable to PDF.
✦ Dealer Assist
The Assist button. Ask it about your own stock, leads and deals in plain language — the Guides panel even hands off to it when a written step isn't enough.

Beyond those, the sidebar's Support button opens WhatsApp to a human. And most screens carry a small Desktop / tablet hint where a job genuinely needs the bigger screen — if a deal or its documents look thin on a small tablet, that's why.

Rule of thumb

Work the two journeys and everything else falls into place: get the car listed (Stock → TruLens → Web), then close the deal (Lead → Negotiating → Deal Readiness → DocHub → Sold). The money screens read themselves once those two are flowing.
