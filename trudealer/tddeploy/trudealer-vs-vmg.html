Bi-Directional Data Intelligence

Legacy systems like VMG force your team to re-enter data between accounting packages, syndication portals, and paper appraisals. TruDealer operates as a unified bi-directional nervous system: when a vehicle is inspected on TruInspect, recon costs and photos instantly update TruFlow DMS. When a deal is signed, Tax Invoices sync directly to your Xero or QuickBooks ledger via Codat.

CPA Legal Protection at Point of Sale

Under the Consumer Protection Act (CPA), independent dealerships face costly voetstoots disputes when defects aren't documented. TruInspect gives your yard an unassailable audit trail: timestamped condition photos, millimeter-precise damage tagging, tyre tread depths, and signed inspector sign-offs on every handover.
