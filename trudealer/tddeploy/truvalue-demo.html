What's your car worth today?

Instant trade-in estimate, verified against the live market. Tap the launcher (bottom-right) to run it — make, model, year, mileage, reg, VIN, condition and any damage. You get a figure and a report; the dealer books the assessment on WhatsApp.

What it captures

  • Vehicle — make, model, year, mileage
  • Reg number & VIN
  • Condition 1–5 · any damage / notes
  • Name & mobile — lead filed before the WhatsApp handoff

What it returns

  • Estimated market value range — verified against live listings
  • A branded trade-in report (link sent on WhatsApp)
  • Always “subject to full assessment”
