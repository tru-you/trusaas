Setup

Phone in one hand, desktop in the other.

Ten minutes, start to finish. Do the three steps in order.

Step 01 · Phone

Shoot a car in TruLens

Open lens.tru-saas.com and tap Try demo. The app walks you through every shot. Add a car, work the capture list, tag damage as you go.

Step 02 · Desktop

Run the DMS

Open premium.tru-saas.com and tap Try demo. The car you shot is already there. Don't just look at it — add a vehicle, move a lead, open a deal jacket, check stock health. This is where the yard runs.

Step 03 · Phone

Write it up in TruInspect

Open inspect.tru-saas.com and tap Try demo. Panel-by-panel condition, trade-in appraisal, and the PDF the buyer signs before they leave.

Install the apps

Put them on your home screen.

On your phone, open lens.tru-saas.com, inspect.tru-saas.com or app.tru-saas.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. They install and run like native apps — camera, offline capture, the lot.
