How much does TruDealer cost?

R1,599/month on TruStart, R3,599/month on TruPro, R3,599 per rooftop/month on TruEnterprise. No setup fee on TruStart. Month-to-month, cancel anytime.

By Paul de Beer, Founder3 min read
Straight answer: TruDealer starts at R1,599/month. There's no per-seat pricing on the capture side, no annual lock-in on the core tiers, and no surprise charges — inspections are included at every level.

What you get at each price

TruStart — R1,599/month

Your own dealer website with unlimited listings, vehicle inspections and trade-in reports (3 templates), guided photo capture with one user, AI chat on your site and WhatsApp, a mobile stock app, all website widgets, and a lead pipeline with 6 stages. No setup fee, cancel anytime.

TruPro — R3,599/month

Everything in TruStart plus a full DMS with deal jackets, F&I and audit trail; unlimited inspection templates; 3 photo-capture users; video handover; live video trade-in appraisal; social publishing to Facebook, Instagram, Google Business, LinkedIn and WhatsApp; SEO + AEO; SLA alerts with AI lead auto-assign; and priority WhatsApp support.

TruEnterprise — R3,599 per rooftop/month

Everything in TruPro plus white-label deployment, multi-branch dashboards and routing, API access, custom inspection templates, unlimited photo-capture users, the TruVoice after-hours AI voice agent, managed SEO + AEO, and a dedicated SLA with phone support.

What's included vs extra

The honest part — what you won't be billed for separately:

  • Inspections included. TruInspect condition reports and trade-in appraisals ship in every tier.
  • No per-seat fees. Capture users are bundled (1 / 3 / unlimited).
  • No per-listing fees. Your stock is unlimited on your own website.
  • Setup covers the real work — custom design or brand reskinning, domain configuration, initial loading of all existing dealer stock, and team training.
