Unified Bi-Directional Nervous System

AutoXloo's platform relies on fragmented modules developed over decades that often require manual sync or separate logins. TruDealer was engineered as a single, modern ecosystem: a change in stock status in TruFlow immediately updates TruLens on your lot, TruInspect in your workshop, TruFlow Mobile on sales reps' phones, and your live website widgets in real time.

Built for South African Dealership Reality

South African dealerships deal with basement parking lots with zero LTE, strict Consumer Protection Act (CPA) transparency rules, and WhatsApp as the dominant customer channel. TruDealer is tailored for this reality: offline PWA capture, unshakeable timestamped condition reports with inspector e-signatures, and native WhatsApp AI that closes deals 24/7.
