Frequently Asked Questions

Motorcycle dealer FAQs

How fast can my bike dealership get onboarded?

Within 24–48 hours. TruDealer runs on modern cloud PWA architecture — there are no servers to install or complex IT configurations. Your staff can start photographing and inspecting bikes from any smartphone immediately.

Do you charge extra per photographer or sales rep?

No. We do not charge per-seat penalties on photo capture. Your floor staff and workshop team can use TruLens and TruInspect without per-user licensing fees.

Can I sell both cars and motorcycles from the same system?

Yes. If your yard sells both cars and motorcycles, TruDealer automatically routes each vehicle through the correct template (28 shots for cars, 14 shots for bikes) based on the make and stock category.
