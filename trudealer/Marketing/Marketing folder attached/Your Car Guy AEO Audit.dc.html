02 · The issues
What's holding you back

Three issues cost you buyers. The rest cost you trust.

Critical · costing you buyers
1
The business is invisible to AI. No AutoDealer schema. Homepage and contact pages carry zero structured data. Engines can't confirm you're a dealer at 17 Burt Drive, Newton Park — so "who sells used cars in Port Elizabeth" never includes you.
2
No FAQ, anywhere. Warranty, finance, trade-ins, delivery, test drives, after-sales — none answered in a form AI can quote. The assistant makes the answer up, or quotes a competitor.
3
Zero informational content. No guides, no price pages, no comparisons. When AI answers "used Toyota Corolla price in South Africa", it cites AutoTrader and Cars.co.za — never you.
High · costing you trust
4 Vehicle schema is incomplete. No condition, transmission, fuel, colour or seller — and "In Stock" is hardcoded even on sold cars.
5 Weak titles. Homepage title is just "Your Car Guy" — no location, no keywords. og:title on the homepage is empty.
6 No review schema. You have a testimonials page — but no stars, no AggregateRating, so AI can't cite "X buyers recommend them".
Medium · minor clarity issues
7 · og:type is "article" on vehicle pages — it should be "product".
8 · No coordinates. Address is text only — local AI can't plot you on a map.

03 · Fix 1 of 6
Fix 1 · The business entity

Make yourself answerable.

One block of code on the homepage and contact page tells every engine who you are — a used-car dealer in Newton Park, your phone, your hours, your location. Fifteen minutes, copy-paste, into the <head> via Rank Math or a code-snippet plugin.

AutoDealer schema · paste into home + contact JSON-LD
<script type="application/ld+json"> { "@context": "https://schema.org", "@type": "AutoDealer", "name": "Your Car Guy", "url": "https://www.yourcarguy.co.za", "telephone": "+27834659921", "email": "sales@yourcarguy.co.za", "priceRange": "R149,000 - R2,799,900", "address": { "@type": "PostalAddress", "streetAddress": "17 Burt Drive", "addressLocality": "Newton Park", "addressRegion": "Eastern Cape", "postalCode": "6055", "addressCountry": "ZA" }, "geo": { "@type": "GeoCoordinates", "latitude": -33.9219, "longitude": 25.5783 }, "openingHoursSpecification": [ { "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification", "dayOfWeek": ["Monday","Tuesday","Wednesday","Thursday","Friday"], "opens": "07:30", "closes": "17:30" }, { "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification", "dayOfWeek": "Saturday", "opens": "07:30", "closes": "13:00" } ], "sameAs": [ "https://www.facebook.com/yourcarguy", "https://www.instagram.com/yourcarguy" ] } </script>
⚠ Before you publish: confirm the postal code, GPS coordinates and your exact Facebook/Instagram URLs — then make the NAP and hours identical to your Google Business Profile. A mismatch makes engines distrust the entity, which is worse than none.
Why it matters
Answer engines cite you for "who sells used cars in Port Elizabeth" — instead of a competitor. Buyers get a confirmed legitimate dealer verdict when they check you against AI. Local queries pull your address, phone and hours — the contact they need to book a drive.

04 · Fix 2 of 6
Fix 2 · FAQ + FAQPage schema

Answer the questions buyers ask at 2am.

A short FAQ on the homepage — each question, one honest answer. Then wrap the block in FAQPage schema and the assistants quote you verbatim. Ten questions, all of which buyers actually ask before they call.

Do you offer a warranty? Yes — with our vehicles. Contact us for the term and cover per car. ⚠ confirm terms
Can I finance a used car? Yes. Our "Check affordability" widget pre-qualifies you in 60 seconds, no impact on your score.
Do you take trade-ins? Yes — we value your current car against the one you're buying.
Do you buy cars for cash? Yes — visit our "Sell Your Vehicle" page and we'll make you an offer. ⚠ confirm process
Can I test drive? Yes — by appointment at the showroom. ⚠ confirm booking
Do you deliver? ⚠ confirm — do you deliver to other cities, and at what cost?
After-sales support? ⚠ confirm — backup lights, licensing, post-sale support.
Where are you? 17 Burt Drive, Newton Park, Port Elizabeth. Mon–Fri 07:30–17:30, Sat 07:30–13:00.
Are your cars inspected? Every vehicle is photographed, checked and graded before it's listed. ⚠ confirm process
Finance options? Bank finance plus instant pre-qualification through our affordability checker.
⚠ Five answers need your policy confirmed (warranty terms, cash-buy process, test-drive booking, delivery, after-sales). We never let AI answer what the business hasn't confirmed — confirm these, then the FAQPage schema wraps the block and the assistants quote you.
