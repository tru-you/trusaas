TruDealer 02 · On the floor
What your team actually touches

One walk-around. Photos, condition and a price, before the kettle boils.

These are real screens from the app your salespeople will use — on their own phones, no training weekend required.

Step 01

Shoot it properly

The app says where to stand for every shot and won't let the hero angle be skipped.

Step 02

Tag the damage

Type, severity and a close-up, pinned to the panel. Scan the photo with AI if you'd rather.

Step 03

Price the trade-in

Fetch market value, subtract the condition, show the customer the working.

Step 04

Send it anywhere

WhatsApp it, publish it, print it or hand over a branded PDF. Same record, one tap.

What the buyer sees

The work you did on the forecourt is the reason they call.

Your TruShowroom page carries the inspection score, the 360° orbit, the damage tags and a TruRepay affordability check — so buyers arrive already convinced instead of asking for more photos. Every calculation lands in TruFlow as a lead.

TruTrade

A trade-in valuation widget on your existing site. The customer submits their car, TruTrade prices it against the live market on autopilot, and the appraisal report is auto-generated — a real, costed trade-in number before they drive anywhere. Zero manual work.

Leads · Pro and up

TruForms

Every enquiry on your site fires two ways at once: a WhatsApp to the salesperson on duty, and a lead file in the DMS with the vehicle, the source and the notes already attached. Nobody watches an inbox, and nothing is retyped.
