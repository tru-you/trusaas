Posting tips (read once, keep in mind)

  • LinkedIn favours posts that keep readers on-platform. Put the cal.com link in the first comment if reach drops on posts with links in the body.
  • Facebook loves images. Attach the TruDealer logo or a live-floor screenshot to every post — dry text posts get suppressed hard.
  • Spacing: LinkedIn once every 2–3 days. Facebook can go daily on the business page but rotate angles so it doesn't feel spammy.
  • Reply fast: the first comment on a launch post determines the reach for the next 6 hours. Have a friend queued up to comment within 5 min of you posting.
  • Repurpose: LinkedIn Post 2 (TruChat spotlight) also works as a TikTok or Reels script — same words, just record yourself saying them over B-roll of the WhatsApp UI.
  • Tag intent: on the UK-localised post, tag 2–3 UK dealer accounts you follow. Don't tag people who don't know you.
