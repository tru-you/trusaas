# TruBrand Studio Pro — Automotive Social & Vehicle Creative Suite

A dedicated, zero-latency creative suite designed for dealerships to brand their vehicle images, create high-converting social media creatives across 4 standard aspect ratios, customize badge colors and card transparency, generate AI social copy, and export clean 4K visuals without paying external graphic designers.

---

## Key Capabilities & Features

### 1. 100% White-Label (Zero Platform Branding on Exports)
- No forced third-party watermarks or platform logos on dealer exports.
- Every exported graphic belongs 100% to the dealership, showing strictly the dealer's logo, colors, contact details, and optional custom trust seal.

### 2. Official 3D TruBrand Identity & Asset Suite
- Includes official 3D Liquid Chrome & Cyan vector brand logo assets matching the TruSaaS ecosystem:
  - `packages/TruBrand/assets/trubrand-logo-3d.svg` — 3D Liquid Chrome & Luminous Cyan showcase emblem.
  - `packages/TruBrand/assets/trubrand-logo-nav.svg` — Nav-optimized vector logo for the studio header.
  - Mirrored in root `assets/brand/` master library.

### 3. Badge Color Customization & Presets
- **Custom Color Pickers:** Fine-tune Badge Fill (background) and Badge Text/Border colors with dynamic opacity.
- **Curated Automotive Badge Presets:**
  - *Brand Teal* (`#0D9488` / `#2DD4BF`)
  - *Electric Blue* (`#1E40AF` / `#60A5FA`)
  - *Emerald Pro* (`#065F46` / `#34D399`)
  - *Gold / Amber* (`#B45309` / `#FBBF24`)
  - *Racing Red* (`#991B1B` / `#F87171`)
  - *Dark Titanium* (`#0F172A` / `#E2E8F0`)
- **Reactive Styling:** Instantly styles both floating corner badges and template card pills.

### 4. Card Box Transparency / Opacity Slider
- **0% to 100% Opacity Range:** Adjust the floating card background from crystal-clear floating text to solid opaque luxury card.
- Built-in text readability protection so vehicle titles and specs remain crisp and legible at any transparency level over background photography.

### 5. Space-Saving Segmented Nav Toggles & Collapsible Workspace
- **Ultra-Compact Header Nav:** Segmented toggle switches for Carousel Slides (`[1] [2] [3]`), Aspect Ratios (`[1:1] [4:5] [9:16] [16:9]`), and compact action buttons (`[Copy] [3x Carousel] [All Ratios] [Export 4K]`).
- **Sidebar Collapse Toggle:** One-click toggle button on the top left to collapse the control sidebar to 0px, granting 100% screen real estate to the canvas stage with auto-fit zoom scaling.
- **Horizontal Segmented Tab Bar:** Single-row pill navigation for Templates, Brand, Vehicle, Badges, and AI Copy, maximizing vertical editing workspace.

### 6. AI Social Caption & Hashtag Copywriter
- **3 Channel Formats:**
  - **Instagram / Facebook Post:** Hook, bulleted spec breakdown, pricing/installment, contact CTA, and 20+ vertical-specific hashtags.
  - **WhatsApp Broadcast / Status:** Direct client message with bold-formatted specs ready for buyer broadcast lists.
  - **Facebook Marketplace / Web Listing Blurb:** Full descriptive listing text with warranty and finance highlights.
- **Copy Tone Selector:** Premium Luxury, High-Urgency Deal, Direct & Specs.
- **One-Click Clipboard Copy:** Instantly copy formatted text with feedback.

### 7. Automotive Badge & Sticker Tray
- Clickable and toggleable badges with crisp SVG vector icons:
  - *1 Owner Vehicle*, *Low Mileage Certified*, *Full Service History*, *Spare Keys Included*, *4x4 / All-Wheel Drive*, *Zero Deposit Available*, *101-Point Inspection*, *Nationwide Delivery*.
- Vertical-specific badges for Superbikes (*Performance Exhaust, Quickshifter*), Commercial (*Heavy Duty Payload, Canopy & Towbar*), and Marine (*Low Engine Hours, Galvanized Trailer*).
- Customizable badge text input and corner badge placement.

### 8. Pro Studio Photo Grading Filters
- Preset Filters:
  - **Natural:** Clean Daylight
  - **Golden Hour:** Warm Sunset
  - **Midnight:** Deep Cyber Teal
  - **Daylight HDR:** Ultra Crisp
  - **Contrast:** Performance Dark
  - **Monochrome:** Luxury Silver
- Custom Sliders: Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, and Background Scrim Darkness.

### 9. Multi-Slide Carousel & Story Mode
- 3-Slide Carousel Generator:
  - **Slide 1:** Hero Photo & Pricing
  - **Slide 2:** Spec & Performance Matrix
  - **Slide 3:** Finance Breakdown & Dealership Contact CTA
- **Batch Export Carousel Set (3 Slides):** Automatically renders and downloads all 3 slides in sequence with `_slide_1.png`, `_slide_2.png`, `_slide_3.png` naming.

### 10. Multi-Vertical Switcher
- Instant toggle between 4 vertical profiles:
  - **Cars & SUVs**
  - **TruDealer Moto (Motorcycles & Bikes)**
  - **Commercial & Bakkies (Trucks & Fleet)**
  - **Marine & Watersports (Boats & Jetskis)**
- Adapts labels, placeholders, badges, and caption generator hashtags automatically.

### 11. Draggable & Movable Text Blocks
- **Direct Canvas Dragging:** Click/tap and drag the template overlay card anywhere on top of the vehicle photo to position it precisely.
- **Precision Position Sliders:** Fine-tune Vertical Position (Y%), Horizontal Position (X%), and Block Width (%) in the sidebar.
- **Quick Snap Anchors:** One-click snapping to *Top Center*, *Center*, or *Bottom*.

### 12. 14 Purpose-Built Automotive Templates (Box & Non-Box Freeform)
1. **Just Arrived / New Stock** — Hero vehicle visual with dynamic lower-third, price pill, specs chip deck, and WhatsApp CTA.

6. **Classifieds Watermark Bar** — Clean photo protection for AutoTrader, Cars.co.za, Facebook Marketplace, and website galleries.
7. **We Buy Cars / Trade-In Hook** — Sourcing campaign overlay with instant trade appraisal CTA.
8. **Showroom Event & Sale** — High-energy weekend event banner with address pin and free fuel voucher/warranty perk.
9. **5-Star Review & Testimonial** — 5 gold stars and genuine customer review snippet over customer vehicle handover.
10. **9:16 Story & Reels Cover** — Safe-zone vertical layout tailored for Instagram Stories, TikTok, Reels, and WhatsApp Status.
11. **Slanted Speed Slashes (Motorsport)** — Angled polygon speed chevrons, dynamic skew price flags, and pill deck.
12. **Minimal Editorial Magazine** — High-fashion floating typography with 90%+ image visibility and deep drop shadows.
13. **Asymmetric Split Panel & Spec HUD** — Dual-column layout with technical specs matrix and vehicle showcase.
14. **Viewfinder Camera Frame & HUD** — Corner brackets `[ + ]`, rec status tag, and floating metadata pills.

## Motion Studio & Short-Form 60FPS Video Creator (Reels / TikTok / Status)
- **Live Motion Canvas Engine:** Toggleable real-time animation with Ken Burns Zoom In/Out, Lateral Drift, and Cinematic Rise.
- **Micro-Animations:** Pulsing price tags, breathing neon badge glows, and staggered typography entrances.
- **Customizable Duration & Depth:** 4s (Story), 6s (Reels), 10s (Showcase) duration options with 102%–125% zoom depth slider.
- **Client-Side 60FPS Video Export:** Direct browser-based frame synthesis and `MediaRecorder` stream capture to `.webm` / `.mp4` video with 0 server rendering cost.

- **Batch Export All Ratios:** Automatically iterates and downloads all 4 social aspect ratios in one click.

Open `packages/TruBrand/index.html` directly in any modern web browser or host it as a standalone static tool. All dealer customizations, badge colors, and box transparency settings are saved automatically to local storage.
