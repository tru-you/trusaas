TruDealer
STANDALONE · no ecosystem required

Dealer widgets that work anywhere.

One loader tag drops affordability, repayment, enquiry, booking and share widgets onto any dealer site. Leads route to whatever the dealer has — a data-webhook (Zapier/CRM), a CallMeBot WhatsApp ping, or a plain WhatsApp/email handoff. Open the console to watch delivery.

Launchers

Every widget mounts its own launcher (bottom-right). Trigger them from anywhere via the JS API.

Lead delivery in this demo

This page points data-webhook at a webhook.site test URL — swap it for your own to watch payloads arrive.
