Our own Check Affordability widget.

Replaces the Find&Drive / Seriti embed with a TruSaaS-owned TruAfford desk — glass FAB, multi-step soft estimate, WhatsApp lead handoff to the yard.

Look bottom-right (above the green WA demo FAB). Same corner as live yourcarguy.co.za — without the third-party iframe.

1 · Soft numbers

Income, expenses, deposit and term. Disposable income drives an instalment band using a conservative % of free cash.

2 · Employment adjuster

Permanent / contract / self-employed softens or tightens the band — still not a bureau score.

3 · Budget range

Shows estimated vehicle price range + monthly from/to, with clear “estimate only” disclaimer.

4 · Lead → WhatsApp

Name + mobile pre-fill a WhatsApp to YCG with the soft estimate. No third-party Seriti code required.
