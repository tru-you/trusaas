What's actually in it

Every module below is shipping code running for real dealers today — not a roadmap. The ones marked with a dot are on the showroom you just used.

TruLens
Capture

Guided photo capture on a phone, with condition assessment per panel and a forced photograph on anything flagged.

TruOrbit
360° walkaround

A real orbit built from frames captured on the floor — draggable on any car, not a mirrored still.

TruFlow
Dealer management

Stock, pricing, deal paperwork and publishing. Premium for a full desk, Light for a phone in a one-person yard.

TruInspect
Inspection · VIR

Structured vehicle inspection reports, in a manual tier and an AI-assisted tier with automatic damage detection.

TruCRM
Leads & follow-up

Every enquiry from every surface in one pipeline, with the vehicle and the source attached to each one.

TruChat
Conversational

An assistant that knows the live stock — asked for a towbar, it answers from the actual floor and hands off to WhatsApp.

TruLive
Video walkaround

A secure link the buyer opens in the browser. The salesperson walks the car live, guided section by section.

TruTrade
Trade-in

Structured appraisal against live market data, so the number offered at the workshop is the number the market supports.

TruPrice
Benchmark

A per-vehicle market benchmark set by the dealer. Where a car is under it, the site says by how much — and where it isn't, it says nothing.

TruSocial
Syndication

Scheduled posting to connected Facebook, Instagram and Google Business accounts, for dealers posting at volume.
