How we calculate the VIR score

The score out of 100 is weighted across four categories: exterior condition (35%), mechanical health (30%), interior wear (20%), and documentation completeness (15%). A score of 90+ is considered Excellent, 80–89 Good, 70–79 Fair, and below 70 will be disclosed clearly before sale.
